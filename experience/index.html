Experience

Selected matters for clients between France, Korea and the rest of Asia. Descriptions are anonymised summaries of past work; outcomes depend on the facts of each case.

Energy, infrastructure and construction

  • A financial consortium's acquisition of assets including 25 solar farms in France, and creation of renewable energy funds
  • EPC project for the construction and operation of solar power plants in the Provence region of France
  • Construction of an offshore floating production unit for oil exploration off the Congolese coast (project value US$400 million)
  • A consortium of Korean investors: financial structuring and acquisition of exploitation rights for a diamond mine in Guinea
  • Advice on a new-generation subzero converter R&D project with EDF.

Digital, media and electronics

  • A listed Korean internet content and webtoon group's acquisition of a major stake in a French company, and its day-to-day operation
  • Acquisition of an R&D centre and French investment, with a capital increase of 200 million
  • Dispute and negotiations in a semiconductor foundry business over a manufacturing defect in a mobile phone component
  • Acquisition in France of a digital camera business from a Korean electronics company
  • Acquisition of an AI R&D centre in France, including legal due diligence.

Fashion, cosmetics and luxury

  • A major Japanese cosmetics group: French business expansion, setting up a French affiliate and day-to-day corporate matters
  • Acquisition of controlling equity in a French cosmetics group, including its production factory and distribution networks
  • A Korean menswear company and fashion designer: French business expansion, acquisition of commercial goodwill and shops
  • Establishment of entities and business structuring for designers, cosmetics producers and exporters of luxury products
  • Incorporation and international business structuring for a wine export business.

Arbitration and litigation

  • Favourable decision in an ICC arbitration against a French defence group over a new-generation Korean submarine construction project
  • A Korean company in a consolidated ICC arbitration over military satellite supplies, against a French aerospace group
  • Successful defence of a Korean media company in an IP and trademark infringement case before the Paris commercial court
  • Advice to the French subsidiary of a Korean tyre manufacturer on restructuring and employment law
  • Representation of the French affiliate of an industrial cables group in tax litigation.
  • Advice to the French subsidiary of a Korean healthcare group on restructuring and employment law.
