    Signature historical case

    The Oegyujanggak case

    Since 2007, James Joong-ho Kim has acted in the claim for the return of Korea's Oegyujanggak royal manuscripts before the Paris Administrative Court. At the December 2009 hearing, the French State used the word “pillage” for the first time.

    See the coverage below ↓

    Timeline from press coverage and a 2017 government case study

    1. 2007Restitution claim brought before the Paris Administrative Court in February.
    2. 2009At the December hearing, the French State uses the word “pillage” for the first time.
    3. 2010Appeal filed on 24 February after the first-instance ruling. In November, the two heads of state announce the return of the manuscripts.
    4. 2011Transfer of the manuscripts to Korea completed in May.

    Sources: press coverage listed on this page; National Human Resources Development Institute (Korea), case study on the Korea–France Oegyujanggak negotiation, September 2017.
