Who we help

Companies entering France and Europe

From Asia, Africa, the US or the Middle East: set up a subsidiary, hire your first team and handle contracts under French and EU law, with lawyers who understand how your head office works.

French and European companies going abroad

Structure a venture in Asia, Africa, the US or the Middle East, negotiate with local partners and resolve disputes across borders.

Startups, SMEs and auto-entrepreneurs

Founders and sole traders setting up or growing in France, including companies in the HEC incubator at Station F, where we act as legal experts.

A French firm with offices in three cities

Archè is a French boutique law firm focused on Asia and on French-speaking countries in Europe, Africa and the Middle East. Our offices are in Paris, Seoul and Hong Kong. Our members have more than twenty-five years of experience in these regions, including at major Anglo-Saxon, French and Korean firms. We advise French and international startups, SMEs and multinational groups, in English, French and Korean.
