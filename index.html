A French firm with offices in three cities

Archè is a French boutique law firm focused on Asia and on French-speaking countries in Europe, Africa and the Middle East. Our offices are in Paris, Seoul and Hong Kong. Our members have more than twenty-five years of experience in these regions, including at major Anglo-Saxon, French and Korean firms. We advise French and international startups, SMEs and multinational groups, in English, French and Korean.
