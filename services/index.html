Common questions

In which languages can we work?

English, French and Korean. Documents can be prepared in the language each party needs.

Do you offer fixed fees?

Yes. Where appropriate, we offer fixed fees for clearly defined assignments, providing transparency and predictability over legal costs. For matters requiring ongoing support or involving greater complexity, we may propose hourly rates or tailored fee arrangements. Our fees are agreed with you in advance, taking into account the nature, scope and complexity of the matter, so that you can make informed decisions from the outset.
