Corporate, M&A, contracts and IP

Setting up and structuring companies, acquisitions, commercial contracts and intellectual property.

  • Incorporating companies, subsidiaries and branches; articles of association and shareholders' agreements
  • Corporate secretarial work, governance and data protection
  • M&A: fundraising and exits, due diligence, takeover bids, negotiation of terms
  • Commercial, distribution, subcontracting, partnership and joint-venture contracts
  • Licensing, trademarks, designs and technology transfer
  • Commercial leases: drafting, review and advice

Employment and professional immigration

French employment law is protective and procedural. We help foreign employers get it right from the first hire.

  • Employment contracts (fixed-term and permanent), resignation, amicable termination, dismissal
  • Harassment, discrimination and equal treatment
  • Transfers of undertakings
  • Litigation before the Conseil de prud'hommes
  • International mobility, posted workers and assignees
  • Visas and work permits

Disputes, debt recovery and insolvency

Representation in court and arbitration, with a partner following the case from start to finish.

  • Dispute prevention and settlement negotiation
  • Asset freezing, provisional measures and judicial mortgages
  • Civil, commercial, administrative and employment litigation
  • National and international arbitration
  • Debt recovery: formal notice, seizures, summary proceedings
  • Insolvency and restructuring: assistance through the process, claims, related litigation
  • Product liability and industrial risk

Foreign investment and international projects

Cross-border work that involves several jurisdictions and several teams at once.

  • Support for foreign investment and project finance
  • Partnership and joint-venture agreements; offering documents

  • Regulatory compliance, data protection and cybersecurity
  • Coordination with local counsel in other countries

Common questions

In which languages can we work?

English, French and Korean. Documents can be prepared in the language each party needs.

Do you offer fixed fees?

For defined tasks such as a company incorporation we can quote a fixed fee after a first conversation. Ask us when you get in touch.

Which office handles my matter?

The Paris office coordinates all matters. Our partners work with colleagues and local counsel in Seoul and Hong Kong when the matter requires it.

Not sure which practice fits?

Describe the situation and we will tell you what is involved and what to prepare.
